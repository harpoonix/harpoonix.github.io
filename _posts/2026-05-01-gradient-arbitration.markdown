I investigated angular and magnitude-based gradient interference in multi-objective reinforcement learning under a shared policy. We formulated a drop-in gradient arbitration method based on a Hyperball variant, improving average performance across 10 robotic control tasks from 82% to 94.5% compared with the best-tuned AdamW baseline.
