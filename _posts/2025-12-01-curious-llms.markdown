I developed Semantic Random Network Distillation, integrating embedding-based intrinsic rewards with GRPO to reward novel reasoning traces while fine-tuning GPT-OSS-20B on 8,000 competition-level math problems. The method improved DeepMath-500 performance by approximately 2.5% over GRPO and raised AIME 2024/25 Pass@16 from 60% to 70%, while lowering token entropy in a way distinct from standard entropy bonuses.
